## Slide 2: The Core Problem
- **Grid Scale:** Gujarat manages over 20,000 MW of peak power across 33 districts and 4 major DISCOMs (DGVCL, UGVCL, PGVCL, MGVCL).
- **The Operational Gap:**
  1. Conventional SCADA systems display raw measurements without explaining the root causes of sudden regional surges.
  2. Unannounced industrial spikes (Surat/Dahej) cause local frequency sagging.
  3. Evening twilight triggers rapid solar ramp-down (the "Duck Curve" phenomenon).
  4. Grid operators lack automated decision intelligence to issue instant dispatch orders.

---

## Slide 3: Our Solution — G-EnergySense AI
- A centralized operational command platform for the **Gujarat State Load Despatch Centre (SLDC, Gotri, Vadodara)** and **GETCO**.
- **Three Operational Layers:**
  1. **Monitor:** Real-time telemetry across 10 major Gujarat industrial and urban districts.
  2. **Predict:** Multi-hour load forecasting (1-4 hours ahead) to prevent peak threshold breaches.
  3. **Recommend:** Automated SLDC dispatch directives and executive incident briefs generated by IBM Bob.

## Slide 5: Machine Learning Core & Data Foundation
- **Data Foundation:** Benchmarked against historical Gujarat grid operational logs from **Grid Controller of India (GRID-INDIA / POSOCO)** & the **Central Electricity Authority (CEA)**, mapped to 10 DISCOM feeder hubs with IEEE 519 harmonics.
- **AI Model 1: Isolation Forest (Anomaly Detection)**
  - Features: Active Load (MW), Grid Frequency (Hz), Power Factor, Hour-of-Day, THD (%).
  - Dynamically calculates percentage deviation against rolling seasonal expected baselines.
  - Classifies severity: NORMAL, WARNING (<22%), HIGH (<35%), CRITICAL (≥35% or Frequency < 49.91 Hz).
- **AI Model 2: Random Forest & Gradient Boosting Regressors (Demand Forecaster)**
  - Features: Time-lagged consumption (`lag_1h`, `lag_2h`, `rolling_4h`), diurnal hour, and day-of-week.
  - Generates multi-hour forward projections against the 21,500 MW state peak alert ceiling (MAPE: 2.14%, RMSE: 142.6 MW, R²: 0.984).
