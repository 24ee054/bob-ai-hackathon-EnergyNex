### [0:00 - 0:35] Scene 1: The Problem & Vision
*(Show the browser on the G-EnergySense AI Dashboard)*
> **Speaker:**  
> "Hello evaluators! Today, the Gujarat State Power Grid operates across 33 districts, managing over 20,000 Megawatts of mixed industrial, urban, agricultural, and renewable load.
> 
> However, grid controllers at the Gujarat State Load Despatch Centre (SLDC) in Gotri, Vadodara, face a fundamental problem: conventional SCADA displays show numbers, but they don't explain **why** demand is surging, **where** localized feeder stress is developing, or **what** automated dispatch actions to take before frequency sags or peak penalty ceilings are breached.
> 
> To solve this, we built **G-EnergySense AI** — an AI-powered operational energy intelligence platform for the entire State of Gujarat down to city and district feeder levels."

### [1:15 - 1:55] Scene 3: AI Anomaly Detection Engine
*(In the sidebar, toggle 'Simulate Surat Industrial Surge (+42%)')*

> "Now, let's test our AI in action. I will simulate an uncoordinated heavy industrial surge in the Surat textile corridor.
> 
> Instantly, our **Isolation Forest Anomaly Detection Engine** flags a **CRITICAL SEVERITY** alert. It calculates that Surat has surged to **4,500 MW**, a **+42% deviation** above its seasonal expected baseline.
> 
> Notice that the system doesn't just stop at detection — it calculates the exact regional baseline deviation and frequency impact, giving operators total situational awareness."

### [1:55 - 2:35] Scene 4: Predictive Forecaster & Operational Directives
*(Scroll down to the 4-Hour Demand Forecaster and SLDC Directives)*

> 
> For our Surat surge, it instructs: *'Mobilize Ukai Hydro fast-peaking units and coordinate rotational feeder draw across Sachin GIDC.'* For Anand and Kheda, it provides substation transformer balancing instructions for Changa and Mogar 66kV substations."
